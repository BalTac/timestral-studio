User Guide · How to Use this Workbench and Interpret Quantitative Metrics Operations Manual

📘 1. How Timestral Works

Timestral is a foundation model trained on billions of temporal data points. Rather than outputting a single brittle line, it infers 21 non-crossing probabilistic quantiles (from q01 to q99).

  • Orange dashed line (q50): The median trajectory, representing the most probable central scenario.
  • Orange shaded cone (q10..q90): The 80% confidence interval. A widening envelope signals elevated volatility or regime uncertainty.

🎯 2. Live Signals (BUY / SELL / HOLD)

The live signal assesses asymmetric risk/reward over the next 12 candles:

  • BUY / LONG: The future median rises ≥ signal threshold (e.g. +0.12%) AND downside tail risk q10 remains bounded above your risk limit.
  • SELL / SHORT: Negative expected return or downside breach. In Spot mode, exits to cash USDT.
  • HOLD / NEUTRAL: Sideways forecast or unfavorable risk/reward asymmetry. Wait for higher conviction.

🔬 3. In-Sample vs Out-of-Sample

The cornerstone of quantitative validation to prevent self-delusion (overfitting):

  • In-Sample (IS - 70%): Historical partition used to calibrate parameters and observe strategy behavior.
  • Out-of-Sample (OOS - 30% Blind): Recent candles withheld from analysis. If a strategy shows profit in IS but loses in OOS, it is a statistical trap!

📊 4. Key Performance Indicators

Standard quantitative indicators measured on the historical series:

  • Profit Factor: Gross profit divided by gross loss. > 1.5 is institutional benchmark; < 1.0 is an unprofitable strategy.
  • Win Rate %: Percentage of closed positions with net positive PnL.
  • Max Drawdown: Largest peak-to-trough decline of the equity curve (risk tolerance test).
  • Sharpe Ratio: Excess returns divided by equity volatility.
